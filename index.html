Dismissal changes

All afternoon dismissal changes go through the KidAccount app, one-time or recurring (for example, car rider to bus rider). Same-day changes must be in by 1:00 PM.

Late sign-in

Students arriving after 8:30 AM sign in at the iPad kiosk in the Front Office with their Student ID badge.

Early check-out

A parent or authorized person signs the student out at the Front Office kiosk and scans their driver's license. For safety, no early check-outs from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM for any reason.

Car line

Display your student's Dismissal Sign on the dashboard so staff can scan the barcode. Without the sign, show a valid photo ID.

Bus riders

Drivers scan each student's ID badge at boarding. Students need their badge ready every afternoon.

Bus Info

Bus Route Sign-Up · 2026–2027 school year

Bus Stop Map Bus Route Schedule Sign-Up Info
Registration is required

Any student who will use the Cornerstone School Bus System must be registered in advance, so we can make sure there's enough seating on each bus. All new and returning riders must register every school year, so please register your student as soon as possible to secure a spot.

Your student must already be enrolled at Cornerstone before completing the bus sign-up form.

Please note
  1. A sign-up is a request and does not guarantee a spot. Bus transportation is a privilege offered on a first come, first served basis.
  2. If you're requesting a bus stop for more than one student, submit a separate form for each student.

Questions? Call the school office at (904) 730-5500.
